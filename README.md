This repository contains scripts, computational workflows, software information, and analysis parameters associated with the comparative and population genomic analyses of *Prunus dulcis* and *Prunus webbii*.

The repository accompanies the manuscript **"Wild and cultivated genomes provide insights into almond evolutionary dynamics and major phenotypic traits"** describing haplotype-resolved genome assemblies of *P. dulcis* cv. 'Tuono' and *P. webbii* individual 'DP9', together with comparative genomics, population genomics, selection-signature, and S-locus analyses.

## Repository structure

The computational workflows are organized according to the main analytical sections of the study:

- **01_genome_assembly_annotation**: de novo assembly and annotation of nuclear and chloroplast genomes
- **02_phylogeny_gene_family_evolution**: phylogenetic inference and gene family evolutionary analyses
- **03_resequencing_variant_calling**: whole-genome resequencing processing and SNP calling
- **04_population_structure**: population structure analyses
- **05_diversity_inbreeding_LD**: nucleotide diversity, inbreeding, and linkage disequilibrium analyses
- **06_selection_signatures**: identification and characterization of genomic selection signals
- **07_S_locus_allele_specific_expression**: allele-specific expression analyses at the S locus
- **08_S_locus_sequence_structure_methylome**: sequence, structural, and DNA methylation analyses of the S locus
- **metadata**: metadata and auxiliary files required by the computational workflows

Detailed software versions, commands, parameters, and input/output information are provided within the corresponding analysis directories.

## Data availability

Raw sequencing data and genomic resources associated with this study are available through the repositories and accession numbers specified in the Data Availability section of the manuscript.
